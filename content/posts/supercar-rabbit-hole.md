It started with one YouTube video - a new supercar release. It was the evening of August 13, and it was the Koenigsegg CCGT1.

Then I noticed another one pop up in the suggestions. And one more, and more. So I realized they were raining down one after another, all launched within a very short time with significantly crafted premiere videos. What's going on right now? Why are all the main manufacturers releasing their new supercars in August? Is it a coincidence, or is there a reason behind it?

I suspect Monterey Car Week is the reason. It is a week-long event in California, USA, that takes place in mid-August. It is one of the most important events in the automotive world, where manufacturers showcase their latest models and concepts. It is also a time when many car enthusiasts and collectors gather to see the latest and greatest in automotive design and engineering.

No commentary, no ranking. Just the videos, one after another, feeding the queue.
